# Open Decisions and Evidence Requests

## Blocking evidence

1. Existing `internal.nklodha.in` repository, authentication and data model.
2. GST live walkthrough: actual fields, attachment limits, successful receipt/reference, replies and delete behavior.
3. MSME ODR post-submit walkthrough: acknowledgement/reference, status timeline, DD payee/instructions, hearing, adjournments and “two-day judgment” meaning. The pre-submit seven-stage field structure is now documented from video.
4. Current WhatsApp/email templates and representative replies.
5. Partial-payment allocation for grouped invoices.
6. Confirm counsel’s position on GST/MSME browser automation, credential vaulting, DPDP notices/retention and debtor profiling.

## Decisions to confirm

- Seven-day GST timer start event: successful portal submission/reference creation, or outbound notification delivery.
- Whether staff must explicitly confirm final GST/MSME submission even in “no approval” autonomous mode.
- Whether postal notice is generated/printed by the app or merely tracked.
- Payment gateway and calendar provider.
- Whether client can upload directly in addition to staff mobile upload (recommended: yes).
- Whether a client’s single user may represent multiple legal entities and who can change that mapping.
- Exact statutory eligibility rules for non-GST, non-MSME, trading, service, export, B2C and disputed invoices.
- Retention/deletion period and legal-hold procedure.
- Whether the universal debtor rating is used only for internal prioritization or also changes workflow/communication.

## Downstream agent instruction

Do not fill these gaps by inventing behavior. Mark assumptions, propose defaults, and return the load-bearing questions to the owner before writing the final architecture spec.
