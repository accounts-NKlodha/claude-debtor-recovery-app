# Product Brief

## Outcome

Recover receivables faster with less staff effort by turning invoice/ledger intake into a tracked, evidence-preserving recovery case. Sufficient authorized input starts work immediately: the platform creates or updates a draft, runs safe deterministic preparation and advances until a named prerequisite or exception requires attention. Standardize communications, GST taxpayer notifications, eligible MSME escalation, payment follow-up and client visibility.

## Users

| User | Needs | Permissions |
|---|---|---|
| Internal staff | Validate documents, run cases, handle replies, payments, filing assistance, DD and hearings | Broad operational access; all cases visible |
| Admin | Configuration, staff/user control, kill switch, security logs, archival/cancellation, escalation | Full access |
| Client user | Upload data, confirm payments/settlements, monitor owned cases and KPIs | One user per client; client data only |

## Commercial rules

- Success fee: 10% of recovered proceeds; 5% for eligible early JITO-member customers.
- Recovered proceeds include cash/bank receipts and TDS, including partial/settled receipts.
- One monthly pro forma per client; final billing remains in Tally.
- Out-of-pocket costs (DD, postage, external fees) are reimbursable.

## First-release capabilities

- Client/staff mobile upload and bulk Excel import.
- Event-driven intake that immediately starts malware scanning, evidence registration, extraction, duplicate/completeness checks and draft/task projections without a staff start click.
- OCR/AI extraction with staff confirmation.
- Duplicate detection and route eligibility checks.
- Case workflow, deadlines, assignments, queues, pause/resume/manual actions.
- Automatic advancement of approved deterministic steps, with humans reserved for exceptions, confirmations, legal judgment, physical work and expressly gated portal actions.
- WhatsApp/email/postal communication tracking through central firm accounts, including a dedicated unified internal WhatsApp/email log with message, thread, delivery, review and evidence access.
- AI reply classification/drafts with staff review.
- Payment proof, client confirmation, partial receipts, promises and settlements.
- Client dashboard and internal operations dashboard.
- GST and MSME filing-pack preparation plus human-assisted browser execution.
- MSME filing-pack preparation supports the seven observed ODR stages and structured claim/document/checklist fields; post-submit lifecycle remains pending evidence.
- DD/hearing tasks, evidence bundles, audit logs, backups and admin kill switch.
- Visible automation state: started time, current prerequisite/blocker, next scheduled action and whether the case is waiting on client, staff or portal.

## Deferred

Native apps, white-labelling, client APIs/webhooks, accounting/bank integrations, elaborate exports, multi-user clients, historic communication migration, unattended CAPTCHA/OTP portal filing and full universal-rating exposure.

## Success measures

Business targets supplied: recovery rate >70%, average recovery time 35–40 days, 1,000 cases/employee, 95% automation. The orchestrator must define each metric’s denominator and distinguish automated preparation, assisted execution and unattended completion.
